## Remaining limits per finding

- **B01:** A finite language corpus cannot certify every utterance. Owners must supervise safety alerts; MeekCall never promises emergency dispatch.
- **B02:** Google cannot participate in a Mongo transaction. An owner can still create a conflicting external event between the last read and write; use staff approval and a designated calendar.
- **B03:** Limits bound application usage, not all carrier transport, number rental, registration or external account spending. Conservative reservations can temporarily deny a call before settlement.
- **B04:** A provider request already accepted before a status change cannot be recalled. New application dispatches fail closed.
- **B05:** Ambiguous speech remains a request for staff review.
- **B06:** Supervised preparation is required for returning accounts without a prepared number. A2P pending still prevents SMS; payment disclosure does not activate carrier service.
- **B07:** Before rollout inventory and expire ALL pre-patch open Checkout sessions in Stripe, including orphaned links. Complete concurrency/resubscribe acceptance in Stripe test mode; no actual charges were made here.
- **H01:** Unknown remote outcomes require staff reconciliation; never erase an attempt to force a retry.
- **H02:** Provider acceptance is not delivery. Unknown outcomes are not automatically resent; staff must reconcile them.
- **H03:** There is no live AI-to-human transfer during the pilot. A future transfer implementation needs its own accepted call-state protocol.
- **H04:** 2557-day minimal financial/release references and a 90-day anonymous erasure receipt remain. Backup expiry and restore tombstones require operator execution. Deleted identity cannot enforce one lifetime free trial.
- **H05:** Existing duplicate data or incompatible index options require reviewed repair; migration never drops constraints automatically.
- **H06:** Syntax/presence validation cannot prove credentials or carrier/email deliverability. Enabled pilot staff email and both SMS flags must be true.
- **H07:** Configure separate domains, databases, Redis namespaces and provider accounts; URL validation alone cannot establish infrastructure isolation.
- **H08:** Use internal scheduling or the accepted Google Calendar connection.
- **H09:** Read-only does not enable new recovery, notifications or other chargeable provider actions.
- **H10:** Live OAuth refresh/revocation timing and already in-flight remote operations still need provider acceptance. Failed revocation remains visible and blocks final erasure.
- **H11:** SMS is still subject to carrier readiness, opt-out and plan/fleet caps. Monitor blocked/uncertain receipts; email and human response are mandatory fallbacks.
- **M01:** Release pins identify the final source pair; the later report/pin-only commit is separate to avoid a self-referential Git hash.
- **M02:** Unsupported or garbled wording requires clarification/staff review, never an invented confirmed booking.
- **M03:** Carrier voicemail/ring timing must be demonstrated on the pilot's actual forwarding setup.
- **M04:** Large/incomplete calendars can deliberately become unavailable rather than offer unsafe slots.
- **M05:** This removes the audited query shape; it is not a measured 1,000-business throughput certificate.
- **M06:** Deploy a generic or maintenance worker and alert on stale success timestamps; code does not deploy or page by itself.

## Test and operational gaps that remain

Mocks cannot validate carrier delivery, billing settlement, a real grant's revocation, model free-text behavior across all languages, deployment namespace isolation or an actual backup restore. The deterministic suite covers the audited hazards and state machines; it does not exhaust every sentence, trade service or third-party race. The 350-session harness tests application isolation/concurrency with synthetic providers; pilot fleet budgets intentionally prevent treating it as permission for 350 simultaneous billable AI calls. No 1,000-business live throughput or profitability certificate is claimed.

Cancellation and erasure are separate actions. Erasure intentionally removes trial identity keys; an erased account cannot be recognized for lifetime trial anti-abuse. Keep this an invited paid cohort and do not recycle deleted identities into free trials. Public self-service trial abuse retention needs a separately approved policy. Financial retention is the documented conservative operational period, not a determination of applicable tax law. External provider history and backup expiry remain operator responsibilities.

## Scope: every admitted business category

This is a shared-platform audit and a trade-fit audit. It is **not a plumbing-only review**. The canonical API allowlist and frontend picker both admit the following 12 categories (`src/helpers/businessTypes.js:1`; `frontend/src/lib/businessTypes.js:1`). A business type does not grant permission to offer a service: the active owner-approved service catalog and booking policy are authoritative (`src/services/trades/tradeProfiles.service.js:4`).

| Business category | Appropriate pilot use | Trade-specific conditions that must be demonstrated |
|---|---|---|
| Plumbing | Repair/inspection requests and approved visits | Active flooding, sewage, gas-line issues; no dispatch promise; distinguish water heater from HVAC; cancellation words inside problem descriptions. |
| HVAC | Equipment-specific repair/maintenance intake | Furnace versus AC permissions, refrigerant review, gas/CO, dangerous heat/cold and vulnerable occupants, seasonal call surges. |
| Electrical | Approved electrical service visits | Sparks, live wires, water near power, circuit versus whole-property scope; no hazardous DIY instructions. |
| Roofing | Inspection/estimate visits and approved repairs | Active interior leaks, collapsing roofs, weather/access constraints; an inspection slot must not imply a roof replacement completion date. |
| Restoration | Damage assessments with staffed urgent review | Active damage versus historical damage, sewage/mold/fire, source still spreading, prompt real staff notification; never imply emergency dispatch. |
| Garage door | Separate door, spring, and opener intake | Door position, trapped person/pet, falling door, no spring/cable DIY; an urgent saved request is not a dispatched technician. |

| Landscaping | Individual visits and estimates | Lawn versus tree/irrigation permissions, downed power lines/tree collapse, hazardous equipment; recurring contracts require staff review. |
| Appliance repair | Appliance-specific diagnostic visits | Brand/model when useful, gas/CO/smoke, water near electricity, parts/warranty restrictions; do not promise completed repair from a visit booking. |
